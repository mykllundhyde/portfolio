02 — THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

One person.
Several tabs open.

I’m interested in how people think, feel and make sense of the world — whether I’m caring for a patient, shaping a visual identity or putting words together.

My background in medicine gives me a human-centred lens. Years of design practice have taught me to turn complex thoughts into clear, engaging visual stories. Writing and reading keep me asking better questions.

I don't see these interests as competing paths. They inform each other.
